03 / Future Scenarios

What if the future doesn’t unfold the way your strategy assumes?

The challenge

Strategy inevitably contains assumptions about the future. The danger is treating one expected future as a forecast and building plans around assumptions that may not survive major technological, economic, regulatory or environmental change.

Approach

Identify the uncertainties capable of materially changing the future, explore how they interact and construct a small number of plausible, internally coherent future worlds.

What it reveals

Instead of predicting which future will occur, management can identify choices that remain robust across futures, options worth preserving and signals that indicate which future may be emerging.
